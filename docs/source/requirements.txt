Heading1: 1. Introduction
Heading2: 1.1 Purpose
This document states what the Orbit Shifting app must do, in a form the development team, vendors and internal stakeholders can plan, build and test against. It follows the App Development Overview deck screen by screen and includes the decisions from the meeting of 17-10-26.
Heading2: 1.2 Product in one line
One farmer-first app on one Orbit backend, with three interfaces (Farmer, Field Assistant and Admin), four partner feeds plus Razorpay payments, Free and Premium plans, and 5% cashback on eligible Shanmukha products.
Heading2: 1.3 The three interfaces
